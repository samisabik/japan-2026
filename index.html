Day trips

  • HakoneHot springs and Fuji views. About 1.5 hours.
  • KamakuraHike to the old quarry ruins, the Mandarado tombs and the 108 tomb caves. Lunch at Juoh Rock.
  • NikkoOnsen town of Yumoto, a 2-hour walk around Lake Yuno, and a free foot bath.
  • ChichibuUrayama Dam and Enyujiiwaido, a temple on stilts on a cliff.
  • Mount NokogiriHike from Hama-Kanaya station. Giant stone Buddha and Fuji views across the bay.
  • Chiba coastOkitsu to Onjuku. Beaches, sea caves and an underwater observatory. Take the Wakashio express.
  • SaitamaHikawa Shrine, the Railway Museum and the Bonsai Museum.
  • Underground flood tunnelHuge "dystopian" concrete halls. Tours need booking.
  • Karuizawa
  • Ikaho OnsenHot spring town with stone steps.
  • UtsunomiyaFamous for gyoza.
